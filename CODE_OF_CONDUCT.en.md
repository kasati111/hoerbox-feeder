# Code of Conduct

*[Deutsche Version](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)*

This project is aimed, among others, at parents setting up content for
their kids – a friendly, respectful tone matters to us accordingly.

## Summary

- Be respectful, even in disagreements.
- Constructive criticism is welcome, personal attacks are not.
- Harassment, discrimination, or offensive behavior of any kind will not
  be tolerated.

## Enforcement

Violations can be reported via GitHub Issues or directly to the
maintainers. Maintainers may remove comments, lock discussions, or exclude
contributors after repeated violations.

Modeled after the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
